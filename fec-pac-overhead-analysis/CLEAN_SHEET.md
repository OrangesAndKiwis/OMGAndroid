# Clean-Sheet Synthesis: What's Worth It, What Isn't

*Two lenses on everything we found — the US Attorney (what's a provable case worth
opening) and the Politico editor (what's a fresh story worth running). Not legal
advice; "scam" is descriptive. Written 2026-07-01.*

## The frame

Screening the 2024 FEC universe on financial ratios, then layering vendor and
donor-side signals, we confirmed a real, large, elder-targeted problem — ~206
committees harvested **~$354M from small donors in 2024** (net of union/advocacy
false positives, **174 PACs / ~$289M**), most kept as overhead. The screen is
sound: run blind, it rediscovered criminally-convicted operations. But the value
now is *triage* — separating the provable/novel from the legal/old.

---

## 🏛️ THE US ATTORNEY'S VIEW — what's worth opening

*A prosecutor wants provable fraud: an affirmative misrepresentation to donors,
scienter, sympathetic victims, a solvent/named defendant, and something not
already charged.*

### OPEN IT
1. **United Women's Health Alliance PAC + "Americans for Female Officers / Female
   Veterans"** — *the strongest new case.* Robocalls ("Emma"/"Simon") tell donors
   money "goes in part to female officers injured in the line of duty"; ~0% does;
   operator **Stephanie Mastroianni pays herself**; charity-name DBA fronts;
   elderly victims; **not yet prosecuted**; a named human with assets. This is the
   *exact* wire-fraud pattern DOJ already proved against Zeitlin — affirmative lie
   + self-dealing — sitting uncharged.
2. **The faux-charity cluster behind Public Support Services** (American Coalition
   for Crisis Relief, American Alliance for Disabled Children, Firefighters Support
   Association, Law Enforcement for a Safer America). One Gelvan-adjacent telefund
   vendor, ~$9M, charity/children/firefighter names, ~0 to the cause. Investigate
   the vendor + the current operators (Gelvan's ban was civil; the operators here
   aren't charged).
3. **United Breast Cancer Support PAC** — pays the *Zeitlin* vendor entities and
   its name collides with a real charity (UBCF). Charity-impersonation on its face;
   worth a look, though thinner public record than #1.

### PASS (for a prosecutor)
- **Zeitlin and Daly** — already convicted. Zeitlin's network is dead (2026 ≈ $0).
  Don't re-litigate.
- **BAMPAC / Elder for America** — real insider self-payment, but **officer/vendor
  salary is legal and disclosed**; without an affirmative lie it's an ethics story,
  not a chargeable fraud. Weak.
- **The "self-dealing" compliance-treasurer hits** (Tea Party/Watkins-246-committees,
  Reform California/Boling) — false positives; routine bookkeeping, not fraud.
- **Direct Support Services** — the roster showed it's *mostly real GOP campaigns*;
  a false operator lead. Drop it.
- **Animal PAC, generic high-overhead PACs, unions, JFCs, MoveOn** — high overhead
  is legal (*Schaumburg/Riley*); no provable deception. Not cases.

**One-line prosecutor pick:** United Women's Health Alliance / Mastroianni —
uncharged, provable affirmative deception + self-dealing, elderly victims.

## 📰 THE POLITICO EDITOR'S VIEW — what's worth running

*An editor wants novelty, named characters, documents, a hook, and public interest
— not a rehash.*

### RUN IT
1. **"The convicted donor-fraudster the RNC keeps paying."** Jack Daly defrauded
   elderly donors (a woman with Alzheimer's), served time — and his firm Better
   Mousetrap Digital has since been paid **~$18M by the RNC, NRCC, and Trump
   committees.** Named, documented, bipartisan-accountability, ironic. *This is the
   most Politico story we have* — and it's a norms/accountability piece, not a
   crime story, which is exactly the lane.
2. **"Emma and Simon": the robocall 'charity' for injured female officers that
   isn't.** United Women's Health Alliance + its officer/veteran fronts, operator
   Mastroianni, the family tie to a breast-cancer foundation. Vivid, victim-forward,
   barely covered. A feature.
3. **The charity-name machine, mapped.** Breast-cancer, disabled-children,
   firefighter, female-veteran PACs that give ~0 to the cause — all wired through
   a couple of telefund vendors (Public Support Services / the Gelvan orbit). A
   data-driven "here's the apparatus" piece, with the shared-vendor map as the art.

### SPIKE IT
- **Zeitlin** — covered to death (CNN, Daily Beast, Jacobin). Old.
- **BAMPAC** — already done (CPI, TIME, BuzzFeed, 2015). Not fresh.
- **The methodology / false-positive nuance** (compliance treasurers, roster
  corrections) — inside baseball; it's *how we know*, not a story.
- **The $354M headline, used raw** — it's gross and includes MoveOn and unions;
  running it unqualified is the kind of overclaim that gets a correction.
- **Naming any single vendor as "a scam" on the concentration score alone** —
  Direct Support Services would have been a false accusation. Roster first.

**One-line editor pick:** the Daly-RNC story — fresh, named, documented, and it
writes itself.

## What BOTH should refuse to do (the discipline)

- **Don't sue/charge/name on the overhead ratio.** *Schaumburg/Riley*: high overhead
  is legal. The case and the story both live on the **affirmative lie** ("your money
  helps injured officers / sick kids") vs. the filings (~0 to the cause).
- **Don't assume the convicted name still runs it.** Zeitlin's window was 2017–2020;
  the *current* health-PAC treasurers (Mastroianni aside) weren't charged. Vendor
  continuity ≠ the same people. Confirm operators.
- **Don't confuse a vendor's clientele for guilt.** Public Support Services also
  bills Honeywell; Direct Support Services is mostly real campaigns. Pull the roster.
- **Mind collectibility/First Amendment/preemption.** Private class actions are hard
  (the one Zeitlin TCPA class was dismissed); the proven winners are **state AGs and
  DOJ**. The highest-benefit move may be a **referral package**, not a solo suit.

## Bottom line
The noteworthy, un-picked fruit is the **charity-impersonation tier** — above all
**United Women's Health Alliance / "Female Officers"** (prosecutor) and the
**Daly-to-RNC pipeline** (editor). Everything convicted is old; everything merely
high-overhead is legal; the middle — *fresh, named, affirmative-deception,
elder-targeted, uncharged* — is the story and the case.
