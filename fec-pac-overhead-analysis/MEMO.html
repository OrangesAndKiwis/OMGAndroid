MEMO — Small-Dollar PAC Overhead: The Waterfall and the Named Groups

Date: 2026-07-01 Re: PACs that raise from small donors, spend almost nothing on the stated cause, and are wired together by shared fundraising vendors Basis: FEC 2024 cycle (last completed); 2026 figures are cycle-to-date Nature: Investigative leads from public FEC filings. "Scam-profile" describes a financial pattern, not a legal finding. High overhead is legal on its own (Schaumburg / Riley); a case requires proof of affirmative deception.

4. Honest limits (read before quoting a number)

  • The screen finds leads, not fraud. A case needs an affirmative lie to

donors (e.g. "your money helps injured officers") contradicted by the filings — not just a high overhead ratio, which is legal.

  • The 41 "newly surfaced" PACs are contaminated. Many are legitimate

independent-expenditure / turnout / redistricting PACs (National Democratic Redistricting PAC, DAGA, 314 Action, Let America Vote) that give ~0 directly to candidates by design and share mainstream party vendors. Their $200M/2024 and $94M/2026 are mostly legitimate political money. The real leads inside the 41 are the small-dollar single-name outrage PACs (e.g. Elbert Guillory's America, Heartland Patriots) — roughly 8–12 worth review, not 41.

  • Vendor continuity ≠ same people. The Zeitlin entities were convicted; some

current PAC operators were not charged. Confirm operators before naming anyone.

  • The provable core is small and clean: the ~23 named-vendor PACs, led by the

11-PAC Zeitlin police/veteran ring, still pulling $11.8M in 2026.
